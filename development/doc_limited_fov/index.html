Limited Acoustic FOV¶

A full acoustic simulation on a whole-head grid is slow and memory-intensive when only the focal zone and near field matter. The acoustic FOV crops the acoustic domain (k-Wave, BabelBrain or jwave) to a beam-axis-aligned box before the simulation and back-projects the result onto the full grid afterwards. A box that misses part of the beam or the focus gives a wrong field, so its size has to enclose both.

On this page:

  • Quick Start: the two keys that switch the FOV on.
  • FOV Box Geometry: where the box starts and how auto sizes it.
  • Choosing FOV Dimensions: what too small and too large boxes do, with starting values.
  • Thermal Grid: why the thermal simulation keeps the full grid by default.
  • Multi-Transducer Setups: FOV boxes for coherent, async and independent runs.

At a glance

  • When you need it: Optional: for large grids, fine resolutions or many runs.
  • Before you start: A working head simulation to compare against.

Quick Start¶

Add the following keys to your study YAML (or set them on the parameters struct before calling prestus_pipeline_start):

grid:
  acoustic_fov_diameter_mm: 60   # lateral width [mm], centred on the focus
  acoustic_fov_length_mm:  120   # axial depth [mm], starting at the transducer

Choose values that enclose the focal zone and any near-field region of interest. The pipeline prints the resulting sub-grid dimensions and offset at runtime, so you can check the crop before a full run.

FOV Box Geometry¶

The FOV is a rectangular box in the beam-axis-aligned acoustic grid:

Dimension Extent Anchor
Axial (beam axis, z in transducer_axis mode) acoustic_fov_length_mm starts at the bowl apex (trans_pos)
Lateral (x, y) acoustic_fov_diameter_mm (full width) centred on the focal point

The axial start sits at the bowl apex rather than at the focus, so the whole near field between transducer and focus is captured; this matters for skull heating metrics. The axial end should extend at least a few wavelengths beyond the focus to avoid boundary artefacts from the PML. acoustic_fov_length_mm: 'auto' sets the length to the apex-to-focus distance (trans_pos to focus_pos) plus grid.z_beyond_focus_mm (default 40 mm); acoustic_fov_diameter_mm: 'auto' uses the outer element diameter of transducer(1). The box is clamped to the full acoustic grid. If acoustic_fov_length_mm is too short for the focus to fall inside, a warning is raised; increase acoustic_fov_length_mm.

Choosing FOV Dimensions¶

Parameter Too small Too large
acoustic_fov_length_mm Focus outside FOV: warning, clamped focal position, incorrect simulation No harm; wastes grid voxels
acoustic_fov_diameter_mm Lateral beam clipped, boundary reflections near focus No harm; wastes grid voxels

Recommended starting values for a single-element transducer at a ~70 mm depth:

acoustic_fov_diameter_mm: 60    # 3–4× the −6 dB beam diameter at focus
acoustic_fov_length_mm:  120    # bowl apex–focus distance + 40 mm (grid.z_beyond_focus_mm default)

At 2 mm resolution this box is roughly 30 × 30 × 60 voxels (54 k), compared with a whole-head grid of 256 × 216 × 192 mm (the benchmark grid; 128 × 108 × 96 voxels, 1.3 M): a ~96 % reduction in voxel count. Print the actual reduction at runtime:

fprintf('FOV: [%s]  full: [%s]  reduction: %.1f %%\n', ...
    num2str(parameters.grid.dims), num2str(full_ac_dims), ...
    100*(1 - prod(parameters.grid.dims)/prod(full_ac_dims)));

Thermal Grid¶

Thermal conduction has a much longer spatial scale than the acoustic focal zone. Heat deposited at the focus diffuses into surrounding tissue over the sonication duration. Cropping the thermal domain would suppress heat loss to surrounding tissue (overestimating the peak temperature rise), miss heating of non-target structures just outside the FOV (such as the skull surface), and give incorrect boundary conditions at the crop edges. By default (grid.thermal_use_acoustic_fov: 0) the thermal stage therefore restores the full grid and back-projects the acoustic field onto it. With grid.thermal_use_acoustic_fov: 1, the thermal simulation runs inside the acoustic FOV box instead; this is faster, but the cooling boundary is less accurate. For faster thermal simulations, use grid.thermal_resolution_mm (coarser grid) or grid.thermal_fov_mm (explicit thermal crop, for prototyping only; see the caveat in doc_parameters.md).

Multi-Transducer Setups¶

The FOV works in both multi-transducer couplings (details):

  • Coherent (all transducers in one run): the FOV box is the union of one box per transducer, each around that transducer's own beam axis, and every transducer's position is moved into the FOV grid. 'auto' is resolved per transducer.
  • Async (transducer_coupling: async): each transducer's acoustic run has its own FOV box; combine_async_intensity back-projects each field onto its full grid before combining, in shared and per_transducer grid mode.

Coherent fields can also be built from per-transducer limited-FOV runs by adding their complex fields (io.save_p_complex, superpose_complex_fields); the sum is complete only where the FOV boxes overlap.

For independent limited-FOV acoustic runs outside async mode, combine them with assemble_limited_fov_fields before thermal:

assemble_limited_fov_fields( ...
    {'sub-001_layered_results_t1.mat', 'sub-001_layered_results_t2.mat'}, ...
    'sub-001_layered_results_combined.mat', ...
    [target_t1_wcm2, target_t2_wcm2]);

The assembler back-projects each sub-grid onto the shared full acoustic grid, sums the intensities incoherently, and writes a combined cache that the thermal pipeline reads as a normal full-grid result. See doc_multitransducer.md for the full multi-transducer workflow.

Pipeline stages, cache and functions
Stage Effect
Medium setup (Stage 4) Full-grid state is saved before the crop.
FOV crop acoustic_grid_fov crops kwave_medium, medium_masks, segmentation, trans_pos, focus_pos, and parameters.grid.dims to the sub-grid.
Source/sensor setup (Stage 5) kgrid and the source matrix are built on the sub-grid.
Acoustic simulation (Stage 6) k-Wave runs on the sub-grid; sensor_data.p_max_all is sub-grid sized.
Acoustic analysis and NIfTI export (Stage 7) Analysis runs on the sub-grid; outputs are back-projected to the full grid before NIfTI writing, so the spatial location is correct.
Thermal setup (Stage 8) Full-grid state is restored. sensor_data.p_max_all is back-projected to the full grid via fov_backproject_volume. kgrid is rebuilt from the full-grid parameters via thermal_grid_setup. With grid.thermal_use_acoustic_fov: 1, the FOV sub-grid is kept and only the T1 ↔ grid affines are shifted by the crop offset.
Thermal simulation and analysis (Stages 8–9) Runs on the full grid, or on the FOV sub-grid with grid.thermal_use_acoustic_fov: 1.

The acoustic cache (.mat file) stores the sub-grid pressure field together with the crop metadata (acoustic_provenance.fov_offset_ac and acoustic_provenance.full_ac_dims). This keeps the cache small and lets assemble_limited_fov_fields back-project and combine multiple limited-FOV results from independent transducer placements.

Function Role
acoustic_grid_fov Crops medium, masks and positions to the FOV sub-grid; returns the crop offset.
fov_backproject_volume Zero-pads a FOV-sized array back to a given full-grid size.
assemble_limited_fov_fields Back-projects and incoherently combines N limited-FOV acoustic caches for multi-transducer thermal simulation.
thermal_grid_setup Rebuilds kgrid and resamples medium/masks for the thermal stage, on the full acoustic grid or, with grid.thermal_use_acoustic_fov: 1, on the FOV sub-grid.
