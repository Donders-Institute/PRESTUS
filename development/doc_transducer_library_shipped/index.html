Shipped Library Entries¶

PRESTUS ships library entries for common devices and driving systems. This page lists them with how well each fits its hydrophone profiles, so you can judge how far to trust a simulation with an entry before using it.

On this page:

  • Shipped Entries and Fit Quality: the entries, their device models and units, drive limits, and deprecated entries.

At a glance

  • When you need it: To choose a device, or to judge how well its entry matches measurements.
  • Before you start: Nothing.

Device Models and Units¶

Each device has a model entry, named after the model (CTX_500), and the library's measured units of it, named model plus unit id (CTX_500_026). A unit's calibration is fitted to that unit's hydrophone profiles, so use the unit's serial when your device is in the library. Otherwise name the model, which gives the model's geometry and a geometric baseline calibration: accuracy is that of the geo column above. A serial of a unit that is not in the library (CTX_500_031) falls back to its model (CTX_500), with a warning; to use the unit's own calibration, add it. A model entry has no driving system; with combo.ds_serial set, PRESTUS falls back to the model's generic entry and says so. Each entry names its model in model:; the naming rules are in config/equipment/README.md.

The model geometry is based on the BabelBrain specification, and for Imasonic on the manufacturer's specification and drawings; where the library also has measured units, the unit geometry is identical.

The steering range (min_foc–max_foc, from the exit plane) is the hardware range of the model; a unit without its own range uses its model's. The measured range is the span of depths with hydrophone profiles in the calibration entry (column Depths mm above). Within the steering range but outside the measured range, PRESTUS extrapolates the fitted model and warns (load_transducer_from_library:extrapolated); outside the steering range it warns that the device cannot be set there. For the Imasonic models, the provenance of each value is in the equipment YAML. CTX_500_006 (2-channel CTX-500, 500 kHz) has no model entry. H246 has flat rings and needs the kWaveArray source (Flat Elements).

Drive Limits¶

A driving system delivers a limited drive per channel, so at deep foci it may not reach the target ISPPA. With a limit, PRESTUS compares the library drive for target_isppa_wcm2 with it and warns (resolve_transducer_from_serial:driveLimit), giving the largest free-water ISPPA reachable at that depth. The limit is transducer.max_elem_p_amp in the study config, else the combo's drive_limit.max_elem_p_amp_pa in the equipment entry. Without either, there is no check. The drive is compared as one value; per-ring gains are not included.

The shipped limits are estimates for the IGT 128-channel driver from the Radboud characterisation (full amplitude for a 1.00 MPa focus at about 83–89 mm): IS_PCD15287_01001 and _01002, and IS_PCD15473_01001. Their source is in the equipment entry. Your amplifier may differ (for example the optional 10 W channels), so set transducer.max_elem_p_amp for your setup.

Deprecated and Excluded Entries¶

A unit that is retired or replaced gets status: deprecated; an entry withheld until it is documented gets status: excluded (both optionally with superseded_by and status_note). Both stay in the library, so earlier studies re-run unchanged, and PRESTUS and the Nexus warn when a study names them. Excluded entries are not listed in the Nexus or calibration_gui and are skipped by build_transducer_library. The OPM variants of IS_PCD15473_01001 and _01003 are excluded until their setup is documented.
